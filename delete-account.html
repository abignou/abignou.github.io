Delete Your Account

If you use the Expense Manager application and want to delete your account and associated data, you can request account deletion by following the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. To request deletion of your account and associated data, send an email from the email address registered with the app. We will process the deletion request..
Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your account information and associated user data may no longer be recoverable.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, the account information associated with your Firebase authentication account will be deleted.

Any personal information associated with your account that is stored by the application will also be deleted, subject to any information that may need to be retained where required by law.

Data Retention

We do not retain your personal account information after the account deletion request has been completed, except where retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

Need Help?

If you are unable to delete your account through the application, please contact us and provide the email address associated with your account.
